# garage emergency unlock - drag chain connector

drag chain used is 10x11mm, with 3-hole mounts.
mounts have M3 holes.
top 2 hole centers are 7mm apart.
center hole is 8mm lower than the top ones.

## parts needed
* M4x40 screw x1
* M4 threaded insert x1
* 10x11mm drag chain + end-connector
* M3x8 screw x3
* M3 threaded insert x3
